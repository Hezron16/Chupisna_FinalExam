About and Help

Our mission

To empower every Filipino with the knowledge and tools to be prepared for any disaster.

The problem

The Philippines has topped the global WorldRiskIndex since 2022, yet many households have no plan, no kit and no idea which agency to follow. SANDIG gives simple guidance in one place.

Sustainable Development Goal

SDG 11: Sustainable Cities and Communities. Target 11.5 aims to significantly reduce the number of people affected by disasters. Target 11.b calls for resilience to disasters.

Who it is for

Families, students, workers and communities, including persons with disabilities, older adults, children and families with limited resources.

Frequently Asked Questions

How to use this website?

Start at Choose a Hazard, then fill out My Checklist, review the Kit Guide and write your Family Plan.

What to do in an emergency?

Stay calm, move to a safe place and follow instructions from local officials. Call 911 for life-threatening emergencies.

Who do I contact?

Dial 911 for emergencies. The Philippine Red Cross hotline is 143. Ask your barangay hall or local DRRMO about evacuation centres. See Emergency Contacts.

What is a resilient community?

A community whose members know their risks, plan together and help each other prepare for and recover from disasters.

Where can I get training?

The Philippine Red Cross, your local DRRMO and the Bureau of Fire Protection offer first aid, CPR and drills.

What sources does this use?

General guidance based on PAGASA, PHIVOLCS, NDRRMC, the Office of Civil Defense, the Philippine Red Cross and the Bureau of Fire Protection. See References. Always follow their latest advisories.

SANDIG

Sandig means “to rely on” in Filipino. SANDIG helps Filipino families, schools and communities prepare for typhoons, floods, earthquakes, fires and volcanic eruptions. It supports UN Sustainable Development Goal 11: Sustainable Cities and Communities.
