Schools and Workplaces

Children and workers spend most of the day away from home. Schools and offices need their own plans, regular drills and trained people. This also supports SDG 4, target 4.a: safe and inclusive learning environments for all.

In school

  • Join the quarterly Nationwide Simultaneous Earthquake Drill and fire and evacuation drills.
  • Know the evacuation route and assembly area for your classroom.
  • Follow the teacher and stay with your class; do not go back for bags.
  • Parents: share your emergency contacts and pick-up arrangements with the school.

Teacher and staff checklist

  • Keep a class list and emergency contacts ready.
  • Identify students who need extra help and assign a buddy.
  • Check exits are clear and marked.
  • Practise a lockdown and an evacuation.

Office and workplace

  • Post evacuation maps near exits.
  • Train floor wardens and first aiders.
  • Keep a list of staff and visitors.
  • Plan for staff with disabilities with a named helper.

On the way to school or work

  • Carry a small bag with water, a whistle and a power bank.
  • Know two ways home and a meeting point.
  • Do not cross floodwater or fallen lines.

SANDIG

Sandig means “to rely on” in Filipino. SANDIG helps Filipino families, schools and communities prepare for typhoons, floods, earthquakes, fires and volcanic eruptions. It supports UN Sustainable Development Goal 11: Sustainable Cities and Communities.
