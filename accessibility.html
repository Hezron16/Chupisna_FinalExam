Accessibility Statement

SANDIG is built so that people with different abilities, devices and connections can use it. We aim for WCAG 2.1 level AA.

What we did

  • Semantic HTML5 landmarks: header, nav, main and footer on every page.
  • A “Skip to main content” link on every page.
  • Meaningful alt text on every image; decorative shapes are hidden from screen readers.
  • Full keyboard use with a bright, visible focus outline.
  • Menus built with native details elements, so they work without JavaScript.
  • Video captions (WebVTT) and a written transcript for every video.
  • Form fields have visible labels; tables have captions and header cells.
  • Responsive layout, readable 18-pixel base text and respect for reduced-motion settings.

Keyboard tips

  • Tab moves to the next link or control; Shift + Tab goes back.
  • Enter opens a link; Enter or Space opens a menu or transcript.
  • Use your browser zoom (Ctrl and +) to enlarge text up to 200%.
  • Use Print (Ctrl+P) for large-print or paper copies of checklists and plans.

Known limits and feedback

Our videos have no spoken audio. Checklist ticks and form entries are not saved when you leave a page. If you find a barrier, email support@sandig.example and tell us the page and the problem.

SANDIG

Sandig means “to rely on” in Filipino. SANDIG helps Filipino families, schools and communities prepare for typhoons, floods, earthquakes, fires and volcanic eruptions. It supports UN Sustainable Development Goal 11: Sustainable Cities and Communities.
